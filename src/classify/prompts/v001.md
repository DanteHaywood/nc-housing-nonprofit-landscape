<category>
<label>affordable_housing_development</label>
<content>Builds, owns, rehabilitates, or operates affordable housing, usually
rental. Community development corporations that develop housing, nonprofit
owners of tax-credit or subsidized apartments, housing preservation and major
rehab programs, and community land trusts that hold rental housing. Not programs
that mainly help people buy homes (homeownership), and not housing whose point
is the services that come with it (supportive_housing).</content>
</category>
<category>
<label>homeownership</label>
<content>Helps people become or stay homeowners. Homebuyer education and
counseling, down-payment assistance, self-help or sweat-equity building such as
Habitat for Humanity affiliates (a ReStore is a revenue source, not the
purpose), foreclosure-prevention counseling, and owner-occupied repair that
keeps owners in their homes. Not general financial literacy with no housing
focus (not_housing).</content>
</category>
<category>
<label>rental_assistance</label>
<content>Helps renters pay for or keep housing they already have. Rent and
utility relief, eviction prevention and diversion, security-deposit programs,
rapid rehousing that is mostly rent payments, and tenant counseling. Not
one-time emergency help where rent is one of many things paid for, such as
food, medical bills, or car repair (not_housing), unless rent is most of the
spending.</content>
</category>
<category>
<label>supportive_housing</label>
<content>Provides housing combined with services, for a population that needs
both. Group homes and supported living for people with disabilities, including
intellectual and developmental disabilities; recovery and sober-living housing;
housing for youth aging out of foster care; permanent supportive housing for
formerly homeless people; and transitional housing with case management. Not
services delivered to people in their own homes with no housing provided, such
as day programs, home care, or employment support (not_housing).</content>
</category>
<category>
<label>not_housing</label>
<content>Housing is not the organization's primary purpose. Most neighborhood
and homeowners' associations, community centers, food banks, churches, general
human services, economic development without a housing focus, and anything
clearly outside housing. Also organizations that touch housing as one of many
services, such as a food bank that gives out rent vouchers or a church with a
small repair ministry. A housing-sounding name does not change this.</content>
</category>
</categories>
